## Formula

`Predictability % = Completed Points / Planned Points × 100`

## How to interpret

Use the trend over multiple sprints rather than treating one sprint as a performance score. Review changes in capacity, scope volatility, dependencies, technical debt, and unplanned support work alongside velocity.
